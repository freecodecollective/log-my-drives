Log My Drives

The easiest way to track teen permit driving hours

Log My Drives helps teen drivers and their parents track the supervised driving hours required for a license in all 50 U.S. states and Washington, D.C.

Free No Ads Private by Design

Features

  • 🎯 One-tap drive logging with GPS route recording
  • 🗺️ Route replay maps for every drive
  • 🏆 Achievement system to keep teens motivated
  • ✅ Supervisor approval workflow for parents and guardians
  • 🌍 State-specific hour requirements for all 50 states + DC
  • 🌙 Automatic day/night and weather condition tracking
  • 📄 PDF export ready for DMV submission
  • ☁️ iCloud sync across family devices
  • 👪 Multi-supervisor support for the whole family

Why Log My Drives?

Other permit hour trackers are filled with ads, lose your data, and only work in a few states. Log My Drives is different:

  • Never lose data — everything saves to your device first
  • All 50 states + DC — knows your state's exact requirements
  • Not surveillance — a logging tool, not a real-time tracker
  • No account lockouts — sign in with Apple, that's it
