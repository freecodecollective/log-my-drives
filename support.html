Log My Drives Support

About

Log My Drives is a free app that helps teen drivers log the supervised driving hours required for their license in all 50 U.S. states and Washington, D.C. It records routes, detects day and night driving, and tracks progress automatically.

FAQ

How do I start logging a drive?

Tap the "Start Drive" button on the Dashboard tab. Select your supervisor and tap go. The app records your route and time and detects day or night automatically. Tap to tag the weather and road type as they change.

How does the app know my state's requirements?

You select your state during setup. The app has built-in hour requirements for all 50 states and DC, including night hours, bad weather hours, and driver's ed reductions.

What does a supervisor need to do?

Supervisors (parents, guardians, instructors) install Log My Drives on their own iPhone, choose Supervising Adult during setup, and enter the teen's invite code. The teen's and the supervisor's iPhones both need to be signed in to iCloud. Supervisors can then review and approve each drive.

Can I add drives I forgot to log?

Yes. Use the manual entry option on the Log tab (called History in older versions of the app) to add past drives with the date, time, duration, and conditions.

How do I export my driving log for the DMV?

Go to Settings and tap "Export All Data." The app generates a PDF with your state's official form name, all approved drives, and hour totals.

Can multiple parents supervise the same teen?

Yes. Each supervisor enters the same invite code. Drives sync through iCloud, so all supervisors see the same drive log.

Does the app track my location in real time?

No. The app records your route only while a drive is active, and supervisors see a drive only after it ends. This is a logging tool, not a surveillance app.

Where is my data stored?

Drives are saved on your phone and synced through Apple's iCloud so the teen and every supervisor see the same log. Anyone with the teen's invite code can see that family's drives, including simplified routes, so share the code only with your supervisors. The developer can also see synced drives. The Privacy Policy has the details, including how to delete synced data.

Is the app really free?

Yes. Log My Drives is completely free with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no premium tier.
