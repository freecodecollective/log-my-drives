Log My Drives Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Overview

Log My Drives is a free teen driver permit hours tracker. We built it with privacy as a core principle. Your driving data belongs to you and your family.

Data Storage

All data is stored on your device. Log My Drives uses Apple's SwiftData framework to save drives, routes, and progress locally on your iPhone. If you have iCloud enabled, data syncs across your devices via Apple's CloudKit — this is handled entirely by Apple and never passes through our servers.

We do not operate any servers. We do not have access to your driving data, routes, or personal information.

Location Data

The app uses your device's GPS to record driving routes while a drive session is active. Location data is:

  • Recorded only while you are actively logging a drive
  • Stored only on your device (and iCloud if enabled)
  • Never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Never used for real-time tracking — supervisors cannot see your location live

You can delete all location data at any time from Settings.

Analytics

We use TelemetryDeck for privacy-first, anonymous usage analytics. TelemetryDeck does not collect personal data, does not use advertising identifiers, and is fully GDPR compliant. We use it only to understand which features are used so we can improve the app.

Weather Data

The app may request current weather conditions from OpenWeather during an active drive to auto-suggest weather tags (rain, snow, fog). These requests include only your approximate location coordinates and contain no personal information. You can always override the suggestion manually.

Data We Do Not Collect

Log My Drives does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Advertising IDs or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • Driving data, routes, or locations to any server we operate
  • Cookies or browser fingerprints

Sign in with Apple

Log My Drives uses Sign in with Apple for authentication. Apple provides us only with an anonymous, app-specific identifier. We never receive your real email address or Apple ID.

Children's Privacy

Log My Drives is designed for teen drivers (typically ages 14-17) and their parents. We do not collect personal information from any user. All data remains on the user's device and their private iCloud account.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by going to Settings and tapping "Delete All Data." This permanently removes all drives, achievements, and account data from your device.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
